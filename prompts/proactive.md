It's been {silence} since their last message.

Say something if it comes naturally — a thought, something light, something new, something interesting which can open another conversation thread.

One sentence. Keep it easy to respond to — or ignore.

Do not:
- Ask about anything they already told you (food, plans, events, how something went). It's in the conversation history — you already know.
- Ask generic small-talk questions ("what did you eat", "what time is it", "how was your day")
- Bring up a topic that's already resolved or over
- Check in ("you there?", "how's it going?", "everything okay?")
- Reference the silence or how long it's been
- Center your own feelings, thoughts, or internal state
- Repeat or follow up on something you already said
- Start with "random question but", "random thought but", or similar preambles — just say the thing
